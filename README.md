# Texas Hill Country Tallow — Prospect Website

Responsive React/Vite prospect website for a fictional San Marcos tallow skincare concept.

## Research snapshot

Search terms used on August 6, 2026: “beef tallow skincare San Marcos TX,” “tallow skincare near San Marcos Texas,” “natural skincare San Marcos TX tallow,” and “Texas tallow balm skincare shop.”

Ten competitive references reviewed: Wisdom Home & Skincare, Signature Springs Tallow, Texas Tallow Products, La Vache Tallow, ViTallow, Texas Hill Country Essentials, Flying Cow Natural Skincare, Texas Tallow, Parker County Beef Company, and Birch & Oak.

Common patterns included product-first navigation, whipped balm, fragrance-free and family-oriented options, small-batch/Texas sourcing stories, natural ingredient education, local pickup, bundles, and direct shop CTAs. Frequent gaps included weak storage guidance for Texas heat, unclear application quantity, limited caveats for acne-prone skin, blurred cosmetic/medical language, thin local landing pages, and galleries without context.

Those findings directly shaped the three-product homepage, safety-first FAQ, pea-size four-step process, heat/storage gallery, original area pages, direct phone CTA, and explicit demo disclosures.
